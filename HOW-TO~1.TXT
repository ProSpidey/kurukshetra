KURUKSHETRA — INSTALL AS A MOBILE APP (PWA)
===========================================
A PWA must be served over http(s) (not opened as a file) for "install" to work.

Quick test on your phone (same Wi-Fi as your computer):
  1. Put this folder on your computer.
  2. In a terminal inside this folder run ONE of:
       npx serve .            (Node)
       python3 -m http.server 8080   (Python)
  3. Note your computer's local IP (e.g. 192.168.1.20).
  4. On your phone's browser open:  http://192.168.1.20:8080
  5. Chrome: menu (⋮) -> "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".
     iOS Safari: Share -> "Add to Home Screen".
  6. Launch from the home-screen icon — it runs fullscreen, landscape, offline.

Or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel) and open the URL on your phone.
