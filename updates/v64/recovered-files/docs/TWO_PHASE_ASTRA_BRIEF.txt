# TWO-PHASE ASTRA SYSTEM

==================================================
PRIMARY DESIGN
==================================================

Every special Astra now has:

1. NORMAL ASTRA
2. SPIRIT-CHARGED ASTRA

Where appropriate, both versions may also support:

- DIRECT / GROUND CAST
- SKY CAST

Conceptually this allows:

NORMAL DIRECT
NORMAL SKY
SPIRIT DIRECT
SPIRIT SKY

Not every Astra must use all four if it does not make gameplay sense.

The architecture must support all four generically.

==================================================
IMPORTANT DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

Normal Astras are:

- quicker
- cheaper
- tactical
- lower commitment
- mostly projectile-based
- easier to evade/counter

Spirit-Charged Astras are:

- slower to prepare
- stronger
- visually distinct
- battlefield-changing
- capable of persistent area effects
- more strategic
- more counter-dependent

Spirit Astras are NOT Ultimates.

Ultimates remain their own higher combat tier.

==================================================
PHASE 3 — CHARGE SYSTEM
==================================================

Aiming and Spirit charging must remain distinct.

Do NOT make ordinary aiming automatically charge a Spirit Astra.

Preferred flow:

Select Astra
→ aim
→ hold Astra-charge input
→ normal Astra becomes available quickly
→ continue holding
→ Spirit meter fills
→ full Spirit state activates
→ release fires Spirit Astra

Player may release early to use Normal Astra.

==================================================

==================================================
SPIRIT CHARGE SHIELD
==================================================

While the player is actively Spirit charging:

create a visible temporary energy shield.

Purpose:

The opponent should have time to recognize:

"the enemy is preparing a Spirit Astra."

Suggested behavior:

Normal arrows cannot deal normal direct HP damage through the charge shield.

Normal arrows MAY reduce shield integrity slightly.

Special Astras may interact based on their normal combat rules.

Ultimates remain unaffected by ordinary Spirit shield protection.

The Spirit shield ends immediately when:

- charge released
- charge cancelled
- heavy stagger breaks charge
- character dies
- round ends

Do NOT create permanent invulnerability.

==================================================

==================================================

Starting target:

During Control Resistance:

- damage still applies
- slow still applies
- push/pull may still apply
- another normal Spirit field cannot immediately apply LEVEL 5 lock

Make configurable.

==================================================
STEAM CLOUD
==================================================

Effects:

- low/no damage
- reduced visibility
- harder trajectory reading
- short duration

The original fire/water field ends and Steam Cloud replaces it.

==================================================

- movement slowdown
- visible electricity through water

==================================================

- light random fire impacts
- shorter duration
- larger but weaker area
