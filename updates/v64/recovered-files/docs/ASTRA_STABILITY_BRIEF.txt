====================================================
2. CORE IDEA
====================================================

Every Spirit Astra has:

CHARGE PROGRESS
and
ASTRA STABILITY

These are DIFFERENT.

CHARGE PROGRESS:

determines how close the Astra is to Spirit Ready.

ASTRA STABILITY:

determines whether the player can maintain that charge while under attack.

Example:

Charge = 82%
Stability = 37%

The Astra is almost ready but becoming unstable because the opponent is applying pressure.

====================================================
3. DO NOT USE NORMAL HP AS STABILITY
====================================================

Astra Stability is not player health.

Damage to the player may affect both:

PLAYER HP

and

ASTRA STABILITY

but they remain separate systems.

The purpose is to allow interruption without requiring huge HP damage.

====================================================
10. INTERRUPTION
====================================================

If Stability reaches 0 while Spirit charging:

do NOT instantly fire the Astra.

Trigger:

ASTRA_INTERRUPTED

Result:

- cancel Spirit Charge
- collapse current Spirit VFX
- remove charge shield
- short recovery animation
- prevent immediate re-charge
- play interruption SFX
- apply configurable recovery delay
- do NOT consume the Astra as though it successfully fired unless existing resource rules explicitly require partial cost

Suggested interruption recovery:

0.6–1.0 sec

Keep configurable.

====================================================
11. DO NOT OVER-PUNISH
====================================================

Interruption should create an opening, not a guaranteed death.

Do NOT:

freeze the player for multiple seconds,
remove all input for too long,
cause massive HP damage automatically.

Recommended:

short stagger/recovery.

Player can defend again quickly.
