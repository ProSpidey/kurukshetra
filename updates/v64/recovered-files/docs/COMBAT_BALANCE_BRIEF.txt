Continue working inside my existing **Kurukshetra Astra Warfare** project.

This milestone is ONLY for the **Combat Balance Rework**.

Do NOT rebuild the game.

Do NOT redesign the current combat architecture unless a small refactor is necessary for clean balance data.

Do NOT add new characters, new passives, new Astras, new menus, or unrelated systems.

The goal is to rebalance the existing 8-character roster so every warrior feels powerful in a different way without one character being consistently superior.

==================================================
PRIMARY DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

DO NOT make every warrior numerically equal.

Instead, balance around:

STRONG ADVANTAGE
+
CLEAR TRADE-OFF

Examples:

High damage
→ slower draw/movement or lower accuracy

Low damage
→ better accuracy, critical potential, speed, or consistency

Strong passive
→ weaker base stats or restricted resources

Strong comeback
→ loss of other combat options

Extremely powerful Ultimate
→ conditional access

Each warrior should have a recognizable combat identity.

- cooldowns

Produce an internal comparison between:

CURRENT VALUE

TARGET BALANCE DIRECTION

Do NOT assume the target values below must be copied literally.

They are balance targets / relative ratios.

Preserve existing systems wherever practical.

==================================================
CORE BALANCE METRIC
==================================================

Do not balance only around damage-per-shot.

Consider:

EFFECTIVE DPS

roughly based on:

base damage
× realistic hit probability
× fire rate
× critical contribution
× passive uptime

Also consider:

- survivability
- movement
- comeback potential
- Astra access
- Ultimate threat
- control effects
- ease of execution

Two characters may have different base damage but similar overall match power.
