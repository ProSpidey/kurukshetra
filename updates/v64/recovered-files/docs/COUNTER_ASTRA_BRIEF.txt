DESIGN GOAL
==================================================

A Counter Astra should create strategic preparation.

Example:

Opponent chooses Drona.

Player knows Drona has:

Agneya Astra
Brahmastra
Acharya's Judgment

The player may choose a WATER-type Counter Astra to prepare for Agneya.

But only ONE Counter Astra can be equipped.

Therefore the player cannot prepare for everything.

This creates:

prediction
matchup knowledge
risk
counterplay

without cluttering combat.

COUNTER ASTRA STRENGTH
==================================================

A Counter Astra used by a non-signature owner should generally be slightly less powerful offensively than a Signature Astra.

Its primary role is COUNTERPLAY.

Suggested concept:

counter Astra offensive damage:
~80–90% of equivalent signature usage

but:

counter effectiveness:
100%

Example:

A player equips Varuna-type Counter Astra primarily to extinguish Agneya.

It should not simultaneously become their best damaging attack.
