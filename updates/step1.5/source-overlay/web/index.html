THE ART OF ARCHERY

Draw. Aim. Release.

Two-phase Astras: arm an Astra and release to fire Normal. Deliberately pull beyond the aiming ring, or hold Space/Enter, to Spirit Charge for 1.5–3 seconds. Spirit costs extra Resolve and creates a battlefield field. Aim upward (65–115°) and release for the sky phase. Charging preserves your aim; no Sky button is needed. Charging has a finite normal-arrow shield; heavy damage, dodge, switching or releasing ends it.

Charged sky barrage: with no Astra armed, charge a normal arrow fully and release skyward to clear incoming rain and launch 10 stronger arrows. A short skyward overdraw still burns incoming rain while charging. Karna’s low-health Kavacha now breaks from enemy damage, not elapsed time.
Field counters: water turns fire into steam. Use Varuna Sky or Spirit against Fire Tornado. Strong wind spreads fire into weaker embers. Thunder electrifies water and can hurt both players. Fire Circle and Nagastra lock movement briefly, but you can still aim and counter. Lower your bow and stop attacking to weaken Narayana. Ultimate counters still require an Ultimate.

Legendary Fate: each warrior has a story-inspired passive. Status appears beneath your health. Karna must tap Repair during its gold pulse four times before the timer expires; he remains vulnerable. Bhishma’s final stand ends in death. Krishna’s automatic lethal rescue shares the once-per-match charge with the manual serpent rescue. Ekalavya may voluntarily sacrifice 15% normal-arrow damage for mastery benefits. Dodge temporarily reduces damage; it has a cooldown. Abhimanyu walks and fights with his bow after his chariot breaks. Further damage breaks the bow permanently: he draws a sword, then takes a chariot wheel as his final weapon. No arrows or Astras after the bow breaks. Tap Strike or tap the battlefield to attack in melee; close the distance with Advance. P1 Z/X walk back/forward; P2 N/M walk forward/back. Space/Enter also strike in melee. Weapon changes never reverse after healing. Keyboard: P1 F repair/sacrifice, G dodge, V strike; P2 J, K, B. Timing and abilities are gameplay adaptations. Fate cinematics do not stop combat.

Phone controls: tap a round Astra icon to arm it, then drag and release. Guard, tactics and movement are transparent buttons over the battlefield. Open Commands for repairs and reverse aiming. Aim directly at body parts or equipment; no target menu is needed. Rescue and dismount appear when relevant. Warriors opens battle setup; leaving it resumes play.

Drag backward anywhere in your half of the arena. Pull down to aim up. A longer pull makes a faster arrow. Follow the dotted arc and release.

Lore encounters: Arjuna vs Karna uses shared elemental knowledge, Aindra/Bhargava barrages, and the serpent-arrow episode. The Narayanastra trial provides 45 seconds of target practice, followed by Ashwatthama’s warning and celestial storm. Ashwatthama cannot be defeated until that storm finishes; afterward, the duel continues. Encounters adapt the episodes; they do not recreate every event. Pashupata remains exclusive to fictional Arcade play.

Counter Astra slot: after locking both warriors, choose one compatible Counter Astra. One use per round, spent only when released; 85% offense and full counter effectiveness. Tap the cyan ⇄ button, then aim and release. Overdraw for Spirit and use ↑ Sky for sky fields. P1 C / P2 H arms the Counter; change keys in Settings. AVAILABLE is a hint, not an automatic intercept. Opponent choices stay hidden until used. Ordinary Counters never stop Ultimates. Karna loses this slot after wheel repair. Lore encounters keep their episode arsenals.

Counters: Varuna extinguishes fire and leaves rain/steam; Vayavya disperses water clouds. Matching in-flight counters grant Resolve. Bhargava clears nearby Aindra barrage arrows when they clash. Steam partially obscures trajectory dots.

Direct hit locations: normal and Spirit arrows deal 1.35× damage to the head, 1× to the body and 0.6× to the lower body. Spirit-arrow base damage is reduced by 36%. Perfect-draw bonuses still apply. The aura follows your warrior’s colour. Hits on the bow damage its string and interrupt shooting for 2 seconds. Wheel hits stop chariot movement for 2.5 seconds. Standard hits halve Resolve gain for 5 seconds. Equipment hits cause disruption rather than health damage. Each part has 3 seconds of protection after recovery. Repair takes 1.2 seconds. Astras retain their direct-hit and area damage rules.

Krishna: Arjuna’s rescue button lights when a special serpent arrow approaches. Trigger it as the arrow nears the chariot; a short protective window lets it pass overhead. One use per best-of-three match, even if mistimed.

Narayanastra: stop attacking and press Lower bow & dismount during the two-second warning. Dismounting takes 0.4 seconds. Remaining armed takes repeated damage; attacking increases it. The invoker cannot shoot while the storm runs. Remounting happens automatically afterward.

Progression: starter Astras are available immediately. Battle techniques unlock at 15 seconds and cost 20 Resolve. Ultimate Astras require at least 30 seconds and 65 Resolve. Resolve builds during combat, through hits, damage taken, and special clashes; using advanced Astras spends it. Progress resets each round.

Common tactics: every warrior gets a random tactic at 10 seconds, then every 18 seconds. Arrow Volley and Rapid Fire arm your next draw; Iron Guard and Second Wind activate immediately. Unused tactics are replaced on the next roll. Keys: R for Player 1, L for Player 2.

Reverse aim: toggle the aim button in Commands or use Settings. Pull-back mode fires opposite your drag; direct mode fires toward it. Both players face the opponent. Aiming stays upward in either mode.

Match: first to two round wins takes the match. Choose the AI difficulty in Settings. Change warriors to start a new match.

Astras: Arcade warriors have three exclusive Astras; Lore encounters use episode-specific arsenals, with separate charges. Tap its circular icon, then drag and release. Q (P1) / U (P2) toggles the selected Astra on keyboard. Overdraw past the ring to reach spirit focus: up to 25% bonus Astra damage. Tap the armed icon again to return to standard arrows. No ammunition is spent by aiming or cancelling.

Sky casting: arm an Astra, aim upward between 65° and 115°, then release. The arrow invokes a ten-second elemental sky effect that damages your opponent after a warning. Fire another non-ultimate Astra into the sky to dispel an enemy effect. Only one sky effect per player can be active. These weather battles are fictional gameplay adaptations.

Astra regeneration: starter Astras recover one charge every 14 seconds; advanced Astras every 26 seconds, up to their original capacity. Badges show the refill countdown when empty. Ultimates remain limited per round, and all unlocks and Resolve costs still apply.

Warrior balance: Bhishma hits hardest but draws and moves slowly. Ekalavya earns the strongest perfect-shot criticals. Karna’s repaired wheel grants permanent pure-archer bonuses for the round, locks ordinary Astras and retains only an Ultimate ready before the wheel broke. Ashwatthama’s Ultimate is counter-only until Mani breaks; his one-use cursed execution still permits the Ultimate counter puzzle. Bhishma’s Final Stand enhances his Ultimate from 72% to 90% of maximum HP.

Rage ultimates: an offensive ultimate unlocks when your opponent has 35% health or less, after 30 seconds and with 65 Resolve. A defender may use a reserved ultimate to counter even if the attacker is healthy. Only another ultimate can counter: choose it within four seconds, then tap the three displayed runes in order within five seconds. Wrong taps reset your sequence. A failed or skipped puzzle keeps your ultimate and Resolve, but the incoming attack continues. Successful counters consume one ultimate. Direct ultimate hits trigger an earth-shattering cutscene. Narayanastra retains its separate dismount rules.

Stamina: Normal arrows are free. Spirit arrows cost 28 stamina; starter / advanced / ultimate Astras cost 25 / 45 / 65. Counter Astras cost 20. Holding a charge drains stamina; sky fire drains faster, and blocked arrows wear down the charge shield. Stamina regenerates after a short rest. Rage fills it once per round. A bowstring break also deals a small hit.

Spirit draw: a spectral warrior, rotating energy rings, and elemental bow effects build as you aim. Fire, thunder, water, wind, venom, solar, and celestial Astras have distinct effects. On keyboard, hold Space (P1) or Enter (P2), adjust aim, then release to shoot.

Two-stage draw: drag inside the dashed ring to aim a normal arrow; aiming alone never charges it. Pull past the ring to deliberately charge a spirit arrow in about 0.65 seconds. Ease back inside to return to normal aim. Release to fire. Keyboard: hold Space / Enter to charge, release to shoot. The opponent's aura and CHARGING meter warn you in advance.

Charging shield: active charging blocks ordinary arrows, including equipment shots. Spirit arrows, Astras, melee and arrow rain can break through. The shield ends when you release, cancel or stop being able to shoot; a held charge expires after 2.4 seconds.

Arrow rain: quick shots aimed above 65° launch rain, with a warning before the arrows fall. Overdraw toward the sky to create automatic fire after 0.12 seconds—no full charge or release needed. Keep the skyward charge active to burn the incoming rain. Full spirit shots can still clear the sky on release.

Clashes: equal-strength normal arrows annihilate on contact. Guard blocks 75% damage for 1.8 seconds (Bhishma: 90% for 2.8 seconds). Karna’s arrows pierce part of the guard.

Desktop: P1: ← → angle, ↑ ↓ power, hold Space to draw, Q arm, E guard. P2: A/D angle, W/S power, hold Enter to draw, U arm, O guard. P pauses. On a touch screen, both players can aim simultaneously.

Online battles: sign in, create a profile, and choose Ranked or Casual. Add friends by Player ID and challenge them directly. Both players run predicted rollback combat over WebRTC. Server-verified results determine rewards and rating. Network interruptions have a 30-second recovery window.

Lore: Arjuna and Bhishma · Karna’s Shakti. Gameplay values are fictional. Sources: Karna Parva counters · Narayanastra · Pashupata’s restriction.
